The products on this site — [agentpay](/agentpay/), [x402 Gateway](/x402gateway/), [x402market](/x402market/), [BSVBounties](/bsvbounties/) — were built with the same pattern this engagement sells: pick a narrow problem, ship the production version, run it on real rails.

## What a week produces

Pick one track:

- **Payment rails**: card top-ups with Stripe, x402/BSV settlement, ledgers, receipts, refunds.
- **Agent interfaces**: MCP servers, scoped keys, policy engines, approval flows, sub-agent delegation.
- **A marketplace or gateway**: listings, verification, pricing, analytics, and discovery.
- **Escrow workflows**: funded tasks, acceptance specs, payouts, retries, and audit trails.

Typical output: a deployed Worker (or two), a shared database schema, tests, docs, and one live end-to-end demo with real settlement. It's a working slice, not a scaffold.

## How the week runs

- **Monday**: scope call and one-page spec — what ships Friday, what explicitly doesn't.
- **Tuesday–Thursday**: daily builds pushed to a repo you own; a short async note each day with a demo link.
- **Friday**: hardening, README, runbook, and a walkthrough. Handover recording included.

You get a decision-maker's calendar, not a discovery phase.

## Why fixed price

## FAQ

### Can we do discovery instead of shipping?

Discovery is fine, but it costs the same week and produces documents, not systems. Most teams get more value from a working slice.

### What stacks do you work in?

TypeScript/JavaScript end to end: Cloudflare Workers, Hono, React, D1/SQLite, Stripe, and @bsv/sdk. The output runs on your accounts.

### How much of the week is actually build time?

Four of five days, with the fifth for hardening and handover. Scope is chosen to fit that, not stretched to fill it.

### What if we need more than a week?

That's a second week, scoped at the end of the first. After a week you'll know whether the collaboration works.
